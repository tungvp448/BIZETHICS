    The story behind the people here

    On October 17th near Bệnh Viện K Tân Triều, local non-governmental organizations (NGOs) gathered near the hospital and brought different kinds of nutritious foods for the patients and the caretakers. Under the scorching heat, a group of women volunteered to help other people who can’t fully support themselves, even though those women were also going through something on their own.

    While contacting different stakeholders for support, we met Ms. X and Ms. Y. (they prefered their names not being publishes) These two independent women started a volunteer group for patients and parents of children with cancer after a family member of their own had cancer. Having gone through the same story with them, we were delighted to work with them, gain access to the ward, and listen to what the families actually needed.
